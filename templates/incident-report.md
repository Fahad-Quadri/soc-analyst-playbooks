## Summary
Two to three sentences: what happened, impact, current state.

## Timeline (UTC)
| Time | Event | Source |
|------|-------|--------|

## Evidence
List artifacts with hashes and storage location (logs, pcaps, headers, screenshots). Note chain of custody.

## Scope & impact
Affected users, hosts, data, and services. Was regulated data exposed?

## Root cause
How did it start, and what control failed or was missing?

## Actions taken
Containment, eradication, recovery, each with timestamp and owner.

## Recommendations
Detection tuning, control changes, training, policy updates.

## Lessons learned
What worked, what slowed us down.
